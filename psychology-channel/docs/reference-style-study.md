# Reference Style Study — "Day One Films" Trilogy

Studied frame-by-frame from `VIDEO REFERENCES\` (Nov 2026). Sample: 15 frames from
DISCIPLINE (157), 14 from Your Phone Is Killing You (200), 6 from Quit Lust (242) —
plus thumbnails, video metadata (all 1280x720 @ 30fps), and the QuitLust transcript.

The three videos share one design system. Treat everything below as the house style
spec for our engine.

---

## 1. The shared visual grammar

### Palette
| Token | Use |
|---|---|
| `#0d0d0d` near-black | Every background. The "void" is the default world. |
| `#f2ead8` cream | The hero character, all narration text, day titles, title cards. |
| accent red `#e8392f` | The Entity's glow + eyes, intrusive-thought text, LED clocks, the underline on title cards, the headband. Red = the enemy / temptation / urgency. |
| warm dusk gradients (brown→orange, purple→red) | Emotional payoff scenes and evening scenes only. Never in the low points. |
| soft blue | Phone screen glow, window night glass, bus seats, notification bubbles. The only "cool" color — always digital. |
| skin tones (tan/brown) | Background characters, QuitLust day 21 partner. Muted, never as saturated as the accents. |

### Typography — one hand-drawn rounded font, three roles
1. **Narration / stat cards** — cream, sentence case or caps, appears beat-by-beat
   (`"But lazy isn't who you are."` small, `"It's what you repeat."` bigger + bolder:
   emphasis through scale, not color).
2. **Intrusive thoughts / temptation speech** — ALWAYS red, lowercase, floating beside
   the Entity or the tempted character: *"why even try?"*, *"you'll go later."*,
   *"just one sec."*, *"you'll be back."* This is a strict rule across all three videos.
3. **Labels / HUD** — cream, caps or semibold: `DAY 1`, `SCREEN TIME TODAY 0h 06m`,
   `MONDAY`, `5:00`.

### Day-title placement (per video, consistent within each)
- DISCIPLINE: top-left, small caps `DAY 47`
- PhoneKillingYou: top-left screen-time HUD + bottom-right day label `THURSDAY` / `DAY 1`
- QuitLust: top-center large `DAY 3`

### Title card formula (closing)
Black field with faint dust particles → giant cream hand-drawn caps word
(`DISCIPLINE`) → wavy hand-drawn red underline → closing lines beneath:
cream line, then the final emphasis word in red (`"You decide."`).
PhoneKillingYou ends with a mock YouTube UI card: `SUBSCRIBED` pill + like button
clicked by a hand cursor.

## 2. The Entity — one shared antagonist across all three videos

A pure-black silhouette figure with:
- **red glowing eyes** (slit lines) and a thin red smile when tempting,
- a soft **red aura/glow** bleeding into the black around it,
- no mouth movement, no detail — it is a shape, not a character.

Its **size is the emotion**:
| State | Staging |
|---|---|
| Tempting | Human-sized, leaning on the chair/wall next to the hero, relaxed lean, red speech text beside it. |
| Dominating | 2–3x hero height, arms spread, blocking a staircase (`you can't.`). |
| Inside you | Seen in the cracked mirror instead of the hero's reflection. |
| Shrunken | Palm-sized, cowering in the corner of the room (the payoff of discipline). |

**Role-reversal is the arc**: the Entity starts huge and controlling; the hero's growth
is measured by the Entity shrinking. This is the strongest single device in all three
videos and both PhoneKillingYou and DISCIPLINE use it explicitly.

## 3. The hero — how he acts emotion WITHOUT talking

Body: rounded "bean" figure — cream, black outline, simple 4-finger-ish mitts, feet as
rounded stubs. The face is only **eyebrows + dot eyes + line mouth**. No lip-sync ever.

The full acting vocabulary observed:

| Emotion | Body | Face | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bored/apathetic doomscroll | Slumped deep in couch, phone low, head tilted | heavy half-lid eyes, flat mouth | phone glow is the only light on him |
| Defeat | Slumped over desk, head on arms / sitting hugging knees, curled small | downturned brows, frown | spotlight cone from above isolates him |
| Tempted | Stares at phone, leans toward it | wide eyes, sweat drop | Entity looms behind, red text floats |
| Resolve | Upright spine, chin up, mid-stride | V-brows, gritted teeth | **red headband appears** — costume state = mindset state |
| Effort | Run cycle with big lean, punch bag strikes | gritted teeth, angry brows | speed lines, screen-flash on impact |
| Anxiety (insomnia) | Sits up in bed staring | hollow wide eyes | red LED clock 2:14 / 5:00 |
| Shame | Stands small before the cracked mirror, shoulders caved | half-lid frown | reflection = the Entity |
| Joy / reconnection | Playing soccer with the kid, big stride | smile, open brows | warm dusk gradient sky, other characters applaud |
| Determined walk (payoff) | Calm upright walk on a ridge at sunset | neutral calm face | the *calmest* face in the video = the biggest moment |

Key insight: **emotion peaks are carried by posture + face + one prop/lighting change,
never by dialogue.** The narration text says the words; the character only shows the
feeling. Two costume states do heavy lifting: red headband = discipline mode,
red beanie = the kid.

## 4. Scene staging devices (the shot grammar)

1. **Void + single ground line.** Default staging: black field, one thin grey ground
   line, furniture floats on it. Nothing else. Cheapest and most iconic look.
2. **Spotlight cone.** Emotional low points: a soft light cone from above isolating the
   huddled hero; everything else fades to black.
3. **Dusk gradient sky.** Payoff and evening scenes: layered hill/window silhouettes
   over a brown→orange gradient. Color arrives only when the mood improves.
4. **Light sources as props.** Desk lamp cone, streetlamp glow, hanging bulb,
   phone screen glow (blue, on the face), TV static. Rooms are otherwise unlit.
5. **The clock beats.** Analog wall clock (Drummond-style minimal), red LED digital
   clock (2:14, 5:00), giant phone lockscreen clock 23:48. Time pressure is always
   diegetic — shown on a clock in frame, not in text.
6. **HUD counters.** PhoneKillingYou's `SCREEN TIME TODAY 0h 06m → 9h 17m` ticking up
   frame by frame is the whole addiction arc quantified in one corner element. Our
   overthinker UIElements screen-time counter already copies this.
7. **Data beats.** QuitLust inserts abstract full-screen graphics: an urge-timer axis
   (0 min → 20 min with a glowing dot), stat cards (`7 HOURS A DAY`), day-title cards.
   These are "breather" shots with pure motion-graphic energy.
8. **Full-screen UI beats.** The phone itself becomes the whole frame: lockscreen with
   23:48, browser with password dots and a thumb tapping. Used for relapse moments.
9. **Inversion flash.** DISCIPLINE flips the whole palette to white-on-dark for one
   punch-bag montage burst (white-out glow + yellow speed lines) — a 1-second style
   shock marking peak effort.
10. **Scale metaphors.** Hero trapped inside a giant phone outline silhouette;
    giant Entity vs tiny hero on the stairs.
11. **Day-title blackout reset.** Between days: quick blackout, new title, new scene.
    Rhythm ≈ 2–5s per shot, day changes every 15–40s.

## 5. Sound structure (from the provided mixes + frame logic)

- **QuitLust** = true voiceover narration (transcript with full sentences) + music bed
  + SFX punctuating beats (phone buzz, footsteps, clock).
- **DISCIPLINE** and **PhoneKillingYou** = no VO track; the *text is the narration*,
  typed on screen to music; SFX (heartbeats, phone buzzes, impacts, whooshes) mark the
  acting beats. Both mixes are music+SFX led.
- Common pattern: **SFX hits land on pose changes** (a thud when he slumps, a buzz when
  the phone lights, heartbeat under the temptation stare, riser into a day-title).
- Our engine already covers 100% of this: `SfxLayer` cues + `Bed` music/ambience +
  optional VO (`tools/tts.py` / ElevenLabs) per episode.

## 6. What our engine needs to build next (gap list)

Already covered by StickGuy/engine: walk/run cycles, wave, point, shrug, jump, idle,
talking pose, palette swap, props in hands, dark/night kits.

Gaps the references demand (priority order):
1. **Emotion faces for StickGuy** — brow/eye/mouth variants: `doom` (half-lids, flat),
   `grit` (V-brows, clenched), `hollow` (wide eyes, tiny pupils), `soft` (calm smile),
   `worried` (raised inner brows + sweat drop). This is the single biggest gap.
2. **Acting poses** — `slump-couch` (sitting, phone-low), `huddle` (hug knees on floor),
   `desk-sleep` (head on arms), `sit-bed-edge`, `lookup` (chin up resolve).
3. **The Entity character** — black silhouette rig with red glow filter, eye-slit
   states, scale parameter (palm-size → 3x), simple lean/block/looming poses.
   This is a second reusable cast member, not a one-off.
4. **Set kit additions** — ground-line void room, spotlight cone overlay, red LED
   clock prop (configurable time), analog wall clock, desk lamp with cone light,
   couch, dresser, cracked mirror, soccer ball, red headband/beanie costume slots
   (StickGuy already has handSlot; add headSlot).
5. **Overlays** — speed lines, impact flash (white-out), notification-bubble cluster,
   day-title component (3 placement variants), stat-card component, wavy red underline.
6. **HUD counter** — screen-time ticker component (already prototyped in overthinker;
   promote into engine with per-frame increment API).
