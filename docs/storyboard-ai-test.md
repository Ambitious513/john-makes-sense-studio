# AI vs CODE-NATIVE STORYBOARD TEST — prompt sheet

Purpose: settle which storyboard pipeline wins for future episodes —
your image generator from these prompts vs `out/storyboard/code-native/01–06.png`
(rendered straight from the locked `DayArcSlice` timeline at the same
story moments).

How to run it:
1. Paste the STYLE BLOCK + one numbered prompt per image (6 images total),
   16:9, highest quality your generator allows.
2. Save as `psychology-channel/out/storyboard/ai/01.jpg` … `06.jpg`.
3. We compare panel-by-panel (composition, readability, emotion, HUD text,
   character consistency) and decide the pipeline for the next episode.

---

## STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)

> Flat 2D vector cartoon still, minimal mid-2010s explainer style, thick
> clean outlines, no gradients except a single soft spotlight cone, no
> texture, no shading beyond flat fills. Colors: near-black charcoal void
> background #0d0d0d, one thin horizontal ground line in dim gray, cream
> off-white #F2EAD8 for skin/ball/title text, alarm red #E8392F for the
> headband/HUD digits, dusty charcoal #3a3f47 clothing. The character is a
> simple stick-figure-built-man: round white head, no visible nose or
> mouth detail unless stated, simple dot/line eyes, dark short-sleeve
> shirt and long pants, chunky rounded shoes, mitten-like dark gloves.
> A second creature "the Entity" is a taller, gaunt pale figure with
> hollow oval eyes, slightly unsettling. Cinematic 16:9 composition,
> negative space, single focal point, storyboard frame quality. No
> photorealism, no 3D render, no anime.

## Judging criteria (score each panel 1–5 both sides)

| criterion | what to look at |
|---|---|
| composition | focal point, staging, negative space |
| readability | can you tell the story beat in 1 second? |
| emotion | face/posture acting |
| HUD/text | digits, titles, captions clean and placed right |
| consistency | same character across all 6 panels |
| motion intent | does it feel like a frame OF the animated film |
